Technologies and resources to promote inclusion for people with visual impairments.

Assistive Products & Resources

1. Screen Readers (Software)

Software that converts displayed text into speech synthesis or Braille output. They allow full navigation of computers and mobile devices.

Examples: NVDA (Free/Windows), TalkBack (Android), and VoiceOver (iOS/macOS).

2. Refreshable Braille Displays (Hardware)

Tactile hardware devices connected to computers or smartphones that translate text from the screen into Braille characters in real time.

3. Smart Glasses & Tech Canes

Equipment fitted with ultrasonic sensors or cameras powered by AI to detect obstacles and read printed text out loud.
